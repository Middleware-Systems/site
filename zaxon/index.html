About Zaxon

We promise to be anywhere for you, whatever you need to ship, and wherever your destination is.

Zaxon Logistics is a logistics and freight forwarding company based in Dubai. It moves cargo locally, regionally and internationally, for customers in many industries.

Mission
To give our clients a positive, reliable experience, to offer real value to the market, and to set the standard for professionalism in the logistics solutions we provide.
Vision
To be an indispensable partner to our clients, helping them build lasting competitive advantages by getting their products to market quickly, efficiently and safely.

Values

  • Respect

    We respect people, communicate clearly and act with integrity.

  • Client focus

    Efficient, responsive and innovative service, built around our clients.

  • Decisiveness

    We decide on careful analysis and on others' input, and then we act.
