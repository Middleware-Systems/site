The number that changes everything

1,000.

That is our minimum for custom printing. The industry asks for ten thousand. We don't.

Short runs are not a favour we do for small customers. They are how our lines are set up: fast plate changes, tight registration, no waste between jobs. So a single café can order printed cups the way a chain does, and a chain can test a design before committing to a season.
