Machines & Capacities

Three production lines for canned, Tetra Pak and glass-bottled beverages: their capacities as the company profile states them, and everything those numbers divide into.

KHS · Canning line

Canning line: 48,000

cans an hour

A KHS canning line with a capacity of 48,000 cans an hour. Divided out, that is 800 a minute: one can every 75 milliseconds, too fast to count and steady enough to hear as a single note.

Tetra Pak · Carton line

Carton line: 7,500

cartons an hour

A Tetra Pak carton line producing 7,500 units an hour: 125 a minute, one carton every 480 milliseconds. Against the glass line it plays 5 beats in the space of 4.

480 msone every
5in each bar

SASIB · Glass bottling line

Glass bottling line: 6,000

bottles an hour

A SASIB glass bottling line with a capacity of 6,000 bottles an hour: 100 a minute, one bottle every 600 milliseconds. Bottlers write that as 100 BPM, bottles per minute. Musicians write the same number for a steady walking tempo.

4in each bar

Together

61,500

61,500 units an hour when the three lines run at capacity together. That is 1,025 a minute, inside one bar of 2.4 seconds. Choose a line to hear it alone.
