B2D is BRINJI Holding's beverage manufacturer in Aleppo: three production lines for cans, cartons and glass bottles. The glass line sets the pace: a capacity of 6,000 bottles an hour is exactly 100 a minute.

01 · The tempo

One bar. 2.4 seconds. 41 drinks.

At capacity, the three lines divide into one shared bar. Every 2.4 seconds the glass line caps 4 bottles, the carton line fills 5 cartons and the canning line 32 cans. Then it starts again.
