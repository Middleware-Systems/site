A brand licensing agreement with SGÜ

B2D has entered into a brand licensing agreement with SGÜ, the owner of the energy drinks Jack Wrestler and Wild Power, to produce their products in Syria.

Company

As part of its industrial growth strategy, B2D has entered into a brand licensing agreement with SGÜ, the owner of the energy drinks Jack Wrestler and Wild Power, to produce their products in Syria.

The partnership reflects the company's capabilities and positions B2D as a growing platform for contract manufacturing, with plans to extend its services to additional regional and international partners.

Jack Wrestler and Wild Power are brands of SGÜ.

Source: BRINJI Holding company profile, p. 10.
