Nearly 30 years in the making.

B2M was launched as part of BRINJI Holding's strategic evolution: a full marketing solutions company built on the group's in-house marketing experience and an in-depth understanding of the Syrian market.

One company for the whole job.

From day one, B2M was envisioned to go beyond the role of a traditional marketing agency.

It brings strategy, creativity, business intelligence, production and execution under one roof, and it serves both BRINJI Holding companies and external clients across Syria.

Under one roof.

B2M's integrated capabilities include:

  • Creative and branding services
  • Market research and studies
  • Printing and production of marketing materials
  • Promotional merchandise
  • Retail displays
  • Signage
  • Shop branding
  • Customised display solutions

One unit at work, one in development.

Business Intelligence

A dedicated unit that provides market data, research and actionable insights to support informed business and marketing decisions.

Market Studies & Surveys

Workspaces and private facilities

A specialised business unit in development, offering shared workspaces and private facilities designed for focus groups, training sessions, workshops, meetings and other professional activities.

A BRINJI Holding company.
