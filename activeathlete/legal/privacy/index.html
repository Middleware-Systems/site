Legal

Privacy

What we keep about you, why, and for how long. In short: only what your order needs, and nothing that follows you around the web.

Who is responsible

Active Athlete is the controller of your personal data under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect, and why

When you order: your name, email, delivery address, phone number if you give it, and what you bought — to deliver your order, send you its emails and handle returns (the contract between us). Payments are taken by our payment provider, Stripe; we never see or keep your card number. If you ask for offers by email: your email address, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. If you send a team enquiry: the details you give, to reply to you.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that help us deliver your order: the payment provider, the courier (name, address, phone), our email service and our hosting provider. Some of them may process data outside the UK, under the safeguards UK law requires. We never sell your data.

If you ask to be told when a size is back, we keep your email for that one message and delete the request once it is sent.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for six years, because UK law requires businesses to keep their accounting records. Marketing consent is kept until you withdraw it. Team enquiries are kept for two years.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

No tracking

This store uses no advertising or analytics trackers. See Cookies.
