03Manufacture

Made to specification, at industrial scale.

We produce sportswear, workwear and casual apparel for men, women and children, to our customers’ specifications, across a range of garment categories.

Our manufacturing facility spans 19,000 m². Historically we owned three major manufacturing facilities, in Jordan, in the free zone and in Damascus, with a combined workforce of up to 4,000 employees. We have completed thousands of sewing contracts, and exports historically accounted for 70% of annual production, with customers in Western Europe.
