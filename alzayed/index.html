Manufacture

Made to specification.

Sportswear, workwear and casual apparel for men, women and children, made to each customer’s specification in a manufacturing facility of 19,000 square metres.

Historically, the house owned three major facilities, in Jordan, the free zone and Damascus, with a combined workforce of up to 4,000 people. Exports historically accounted for 70% of annual production, with customers in Western Europe.

Three ways of working

  • RMGReady-made garments: the garment delivered finished.
  • CMTCut, make and trim: cut, sewn and finished to the customer’s specification.
  • CMCut and make: the customer supplies cloth and trims; we cut and sew.

Thousands of sewing contracts completed.

A French brand, in the house since 2012.

Acquired in 2012 by founder Majed Alzayed. Active Athlete previously supplied and worked with sports clubs in the Gulf and in Europe. Today it offers monogram services and business-to-business supply, sponsors numerous teams in Syria, and supplies customers in Greece, Tanzania, the United Kingdom and France.

Industry

Yeast, for the country’s bread.

After three government-owned yeast factories were destroyed during the war, the house established a private baker’s yeast factory in Syria in 2015, to help meet domestic needs.

6,000Tons of fresh yeast a year: the factory’s recorded production capacity

Humanitarian supply

Beside those who serve people in need, since 2010.

Through tenders, the house supplies United Nations organisations operating locally, and NGOs, with school bags, uniforms, seasonal apparel, hygiene items and food assistance. Within the first two years, it became a major supplier to these organisations in these categories.

Property & construction

Malls, land and homes.

The house’s work extends to building and construction, property development and real estate investment. Its portfolio has included an operating shopping mall, a second mall under construction, land holdings and residential apartment buildings.
