The identity · a proposal

For Majed & Mohamed Alzayed Trading and Industry Co. Nothing here is adopted until they say so.

01From their logo

We kept their Z and its two diagonals. Everything else went.

Their logo is a large grey Z that holds the names Majed and Mohamed, with two parallel bars, one red and one blue, inside its diagonal. The new mark is that Z alone, drawn as a letter: the Z of Noto Serif Display, a Didone whose Z already has hairline bars and one heavy diagonal, with the diagonal split into two parallel strokes. Two strokes, one direction: the two names the company carries; and the two wales of a Z-twill, the weave of the world's workwear.

02The mark, measured

A letter, cut once.

The slit is computed from the glyph's own diagonal edges and wound against its outline, so it is a true opening in any file and the two strokes meet the hairlines exactly where the letter's diagonal does. Units are the font's (1,000 to the em). The woven version thickens the hairlines to whole threads and cuts the slit again: a loom cannot weave a hairline.

03Signatures

The mark stands alone or above the name, never beside it.

A Z beside a name that holds a Z reads "Z ALZAYED". So: the signature (the name over its descriptor), the stack (the mark over the name), and the mark by itself for small places. The name's own Z carries the same slit.

04Colour

Six colours, each from their logo.

Their navy, their red and their grey, taken into cloth: the navy to the depth of indigo-dyed cloth, the grey warmed to undyed greige, and the red kept as one thread, the way a mill weaves a coloured ID line into a selvedge. Red is never text.

06The house cloth

A Z-twill with the mark woven in, shown only by the light.

The ground is a warp-faced Z-twill; the mark is woven into it in a weft-faced sateen, as damask is, in the same colour. Under a still light it is almost plain cloth; as the light moves the marks turn from dark to bright. On the site it is drawn live, thread by thread; here, three colourways from the same drawing.
