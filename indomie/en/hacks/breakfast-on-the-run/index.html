Breakfast on the Run

Labneh and za’atar, but hot.

Serves 1 Mild

What you need

  • 1 pack of Indomie
  • 2 tablespoons of labneh
  • 1 tablespoon of olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon of za’atar
  • a pinch of dried mint
  • a few slices of cucumber
  • a few olives

How

  1. Start
    Cook the noodles as the pack says, with half the seasoning.
  2. At the end
    Drain, leaving a spoonful of the water in the bowl.
  3. 15 sec after the end
    Spoon the labneh on one side, pour the oil over it, then scatter the za’atar and mint over everything.
  4. 30 sec after the end
    Cucumber and olives beside. Mix a little into every forkful.

Why it works

It is the Syrian breakfast plate, labneh, oil and za’atar, with hot noodles where the bread would be: their heat loosens the labneh into a sauce.
