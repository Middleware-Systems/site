Make the Falafel Jealous

All the tarator, without leaving the house.

2 min prep Serves 1 Mild

What you need

  • 1 pack of Indomie
  • 2 tablespoons of tahini
  • the juice of half a lemon
  • 1 small garlic clove, crushed
  • cold water
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • a few slices of pickled turnip
  • parsley
  • sumac

How

  1. Before
    Whisk the tahini with the lemon and garlic, adding cold water a spoon at a time until it is like thick cream: that is the tarator.
  2. Start
    Cook the noodles as the pack says.
  3. At the end
    Drain, then rinse under the cold tap so they don’t stick.
  4. 30 sec after the end
    Toss with the tarator and a quarter of the seasoning.
  5. 1 min after the end
    Top with tomato, turnip, parsley and sumac. Eat it warm or cold.

Why it works

Rinsed noodles stay separate and take the tahini like a dressing, so the bowl tastes like the falafel sandwich.
