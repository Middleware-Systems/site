The House Pot

One pot. Enough for everyone.

10 min prep Serves 4 Mild

What you need

  • 4 packs of Indomie
  • 1 tablespoon of oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 courgette, diced
  • 1 cup of peas (frozen is fine)
  • leftover cooked chicken, shredded (optional)
  • water: four times what one pack asks for, plus one cup
  • lemon wedges

How

  1. Before
    Soften the onion in the oil in a large pot for 3 minutes, then the carrot and courgette for 2 more.
  2. Before
    Add the water and all the seasoning, bring it to the boil and let it bubble for 5 minutes.
  3. Start
    Add the four noodle blocks and the peas (and the chicken).
  4. At the end
    Turn off the heat as soon as the noodles are done: they keep softening in the pot.
  5. 30 sec after the end
    Ladle into bowls, with a squeeze of lemon in each.

Why it works

Cooking the vegetables first in the seasoned broth turns four packs into a real family soup, and the lemon at the table keeps it bright.
