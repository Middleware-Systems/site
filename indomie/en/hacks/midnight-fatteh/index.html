Midnight Fatteh

Yesterday’s bread, yogurt and Indomie. That’s fatteh.

4 min prep Serves 2 Mild

What you need

  • 2 packs of Indomie
  • 1 loaf of yesterday’s Arabic bread
  • 1 cup of plain yogurt, at room temperature
  • 1 small garlic clove, crushed with a pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of ghee or butter
  • a handful of pine nuts or almonds (optional)
  • sumac or paprika, and chopped parsley

How

  1. Before
    Tear the bread into small pieces and toast them in a dry pan until they snap.
  2. Before
    Beat the yogurt with the garlic.
  3. Start
    Cook the noodles as the pack says, with half the seasoning.
  4. At the end
    Drain, keeping half a cup of the cooking water.
  5. 30 sec after the end
    Layer: bread on the plate, a splash of cooking water over it, then the noodles, then the yogurt.
  6. 1½ min after the end
    Fry the nuts in the ghee until golden, and pour it all, sizzling, over the top. Sumac and parsley to finish.

Why it works

The salty cooking water softens the bread underneath while the top stays crisp, and hot ghee over cool yogurt is the contrast every fatteh is built on.

Careful

Mind the hot ghee as you pour it.
