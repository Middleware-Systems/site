Midnight Fatteh

Yesterday’s bread, yogurt and Indomie. That’s fatteh.

Serves
2
Taste
Mild
Cook
3 minutes + 4 min

You need

  • 2 packs of Indomie
  • 1 loaf of yesterday’s Arabic bread
  • 1 cup of plain yogurt, at room temperature
  • 1 small garlic clove, crushed with a pinch of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of ghee or butter
  • a handful of pine nuts or almonds (optional)
  • sumac or paprika, and chopped parsley

Method

  1. Before you startTear the bread into small pieces and toast them in a dry pan until they snap.
  2. Before you startBeat the yogurt with the garlic.
  3. At the startCook the noodles as the pack says, with half the seasoning.
  4. At the endDrain, keeping half a cup of the cooking water.
  5. 30 seconds after the endLayer: bread on the plate, a splash of cooking water over it, then the noodles, then the yogurt.
  6. 1½ minutes after the endFry the nuts in the ghee until golden, and pour it all, sizzling, over the top. Sumac and parsley to finish.

Why it works

The salty cooking water softens the bread underneath while the top stays crisp, and hot ghee over cool yogurt is the contrast every fatteh is built on.

Careful

Mind the hot ghee as you pour it.
