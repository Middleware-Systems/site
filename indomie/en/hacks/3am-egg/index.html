The 3 a.m. Egg

A hot bowl, then bed.

Serves 1 A kick

What you need

  • 1 pack of Indomie
  • 1 egg
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • a pinch of Aleppo pepper flakes or chili flakes

How

  1. Before
    Bring the water to the boil in a small pot, in the amount the pack gives.
  2. Start
    Add the noodle block.
  3. 1 min before the end
    Stir in the seasoning, then turn the heat down until the water barely moves.
  4. 1 min before the end
    Crack the egg into the middle without stirring, and cover the pot.
  5. At the end
    Turn off the heat and leave the lid on for one more minute: the white sets and the yolk stays soft.
  6. 1 min after the end
    Slide it into a bowl, then the spring onion and the pepper on top.

Why it works

The egg poaches in the seasoned broth instead of a pan, so there is one pot to wash, and the yolk thickens the soup with every spoonful.
