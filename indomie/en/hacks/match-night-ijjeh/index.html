Match-Night Ijjeh

Three eggs and a pack. Dinner for everyone.

Serves 3 A kick

What you need

  • 1 pack of Indomie
  • 3 eggs
  • a handful of parsley, chopped
  • 2 spring onions, chopped
  • ½ teaspoon of the seasoning
  • 1 tablespoon of flour (optional, for a firmer ijjeh)
  • 2 tablespoons of oil
  • pickles and bread to serve

How

  1. Start
    Cook the noodles for one minute less than the pack says: they finish in the pan.
  2. 1 min before the end
    Drain well and let them steam dry for a minute.
  3. At the end
    Beat the eggs with the herbs, the seasoning and the flour, then fold in the noodles.
  4. 1 min after the end
    Heat the oil in a pan, pour everything in, flatten it, and cook on medium heat for 4 minutes until the base is golden.
  5. 5 min after the end
    Turn it with a plate (slide it out, flip it, slide it back) and cook 3 minutes more.
  6. 8 min after the end
    Cut it into wedges and serve with pickles.

Why it works

Noodles taken off a minute early bind with the egg instead of going soft, so the ijjeh cuts into clean wedges you can eat by hand in front of the match.

Careful

Mind the hot oil when you turn it.
