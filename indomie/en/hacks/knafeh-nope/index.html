Knafeh? Nope. Indomie.

Something sweet before sunrise.

Serves
4
Taste
Sweet
Cook
3 minutes + 20 min

You need

  • 2 packs of Indomie (keep the seasoning for another day)
  • 3 tablespoons of melted ghee or butter
  • 1 cup of ashta, or a mild white cheese that melts, soaked first to take out the salt
  • ground pistachios
  • for the syrup: ½ cup of sugar, a third of a cup of water, a few drops of lemon, 1 teaspoon of orange-blossom water

Method

  1. Before you startMake the syrup: boil the sugar and water for 5 minutes, add the lemon and orange blossom, and let it cool for at least 15 minutes.
  2. At the startCook the noodles for half the time on the pack, with no seasoning.
  3. HalfwayDrain very well and toss with the melted ghee.
  4. Halfway + 1 minutePress half into a small non-stick pan, spread the ashta over it, cover with the rest and press firmly.
  5. Halfway + 2 minutesCook on low heat for 6 to 8 minutes until the base is deep golden, then turn it with a plate and colour the other side.
  6. Halfway + 14 minutesPour the cold syrup over the hot knafeh, and finish with the pistachios.

Why it works

Cold syrup on hot pastry is the first rule of knafeh: the top drinks it in without losing its crunch.

Careful

Mind the hot ghee when you turn it.
