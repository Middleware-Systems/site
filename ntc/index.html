The name

Our name is the word for dry.

In Arabic, nashifa is to dry, and a towel is a minshafa — grown from the same three letters as Alnashef.

The yarn

Twisted, or single.

Our heavy sets, 01 to 06, are woven from two-ply yarn: two strands twisted into one before the loom. Sets 07 to 09 are woven from single yarn, and 09 is our light set.
