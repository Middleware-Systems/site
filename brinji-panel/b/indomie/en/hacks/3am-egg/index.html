󠁛󠀸󠁶󠁝The 3 a.m. Egg󠁻󠀸󠁶󠁽

󠁛󠀸󠁸󠁝A hot bowl, then bed.󠁻󠀸󠁸󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠀷󠁝Serves󠁻󠀳󠀷󠁽
1
Taste
󠁛󠀳󠁬󠁝A little kick󠁻󠀳󠁬󠁽
Cook
󠁛󠀷󠀹󠁝3 minutes󠁻󠀷󠀹󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁰󠁝You need󠁻󠀳󠁰󠁽

  • 󠁛󠀸󠁺󠁝1 pack of Indomie󠁻󠀸󠁺󠁽
  • 󠁛󠀹󠀱󠁝1 egg󠁻󠀹󠀱󠁽
  • 󠁛󠀹󠀳󠁝1 spring onion, sliced󠁻󠀹󠀳󠁽
  • 󠁛󠀹󠀵󠁝a pinch of Aleppo pepper flakes or chili flakes󠁻󠀹󠀵󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁲󠁝Method󠁻󠀳󠁲󠁽

  1. 󠁛󠀴󠀳󠁝Before you start󠁻󠀴󠀳󠁽󠁛󠀹󠀷󠁝Bring the water to the boil in a small pot, in the amount the pack gives.󠁻󠀹󠀷󠁽
  2. 󠁛󠀴󠀵󠁝At the start󠁻󠀴󠀵󠁽󠁛󠀹󠀹󠁝Add the noodle block.󠁻󠀹󠀹󠁽
  3. 󠁛󠀴󠁢󠁝󠁛󠀴󠁪󠁝1 minute󠁻󠀴󠁪󠁽 before the end󠁻󠀴󠁢󠁽󠁛󠀹󠁢󠁝Stir in the seasoning, then turn the heat down until the water barely moves.󠁻󠀹󠁢󠁽
  4. 󠁛󠀴󠁢󠁝󠁛󠀴󠁪󠁝1 minute󠁻󠀴󠁪󠁽 before the end󠁻󠀴󠁢󠁽󠁛󠀹󠁤󠁝Crack the egg into the middle without stirring, and cover the pot.󠁻󠀹󠁤󠁽
  5. 󠁛󠀴󠀹󠁝At the end󠁻󠀴󠀹󠁽󠁛󠀹󠁦󠁝Turn off the heat and leave the lid on for one more minute: the white sets and the yolk stays soft.󠁻󠀹󠁦󠁽
  6. 󠁛󠀴󠁤󠁝󠁛󠀴󠁪󠁝1 minute󠁻󠀴󠁪󠁽 after the end󠁻󠀴󠁤󠁽󠁛󠀹󠁨󠁝Slide it into a bowl, then the spring onion and the pepper on top.󠁻󠀹󠁨󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁴󠁝Why it works󠁻󠀳󠁴󠁽

󠁛󠀹󠁪󠁝The egg poaches in the seasoned broth instead of a pan, so there is one pot to wash, and the yolk thickens the soup with every spoonful.󠁻󠀹󠁪󠁽
