󠁛󠁣󠁤󠁝Make the Falafel Jealous󠁻󠁣󠁤󠁽

󠁛󠁣󠁦󠁝All the tarator, without leaving the house.󠁻󠁣󠁦󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠀷󠁝Serves󠁻󠀳󠀷󠁽
1
Taste
󠁛󠀳󠁪󠁝Mild󠁻󠀳󠁪󠁽
Cook
󠁛󠀷󠀹󠁝3 minutes󠁻󠀷󠀹󠁽 + 2 󠁛󠀳󠁦󠁝min󠁻󠀳󠁦󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁰󠁝You need󠁻󠀳󠁰󠁽

  • 󠁛󠁣󠁨󠁝1 pack of Indomie󠁻󠁣󠁨󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁪󠁝2 tablespoons of tahini󠁻󠁣󠁪󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁬󠁝the juice of half a lemon󠁻󠁣󠁬󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁮󠁝1 small garlic clove, crushed󠁻󠁣󠁮󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁰󠁝cold water󠁻󠁣󠁰󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁲󠁝1 tomato, diced󠁻󠁣󠁲󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁴󠁝a few slices of pickled turnip󠁻󠁣󠁴󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁶󠁝parsley󠁻󠁣󠁶󠁽
  • 󠁛󠁣󠁸󠁝sumac󠁻󠁣󠁸󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁲󠁝Method󠁻󠀳󠁲󠁽

  1. 󠁛󠀴󠀳󠁝Before you start󠁻󠀴󠀳󠁽󠁛󠁣󠁺󠁝Whisk the tahini with the lemon and garlic, adding cold water a spoon at a time until it is like thick cream: that is the tarator.󠁻󠁣󠁺󠁽
  2. 󠁛󠀴󠀵󠁝At the start󠁻󠀴󠀵󠁽󠁛󠁤󠀱󠁝Cook the noodles as the pack says.󠁻󠁤󠀱󠁽
  3. 󠁛󠀴󠀹󠁝At the end󠁻󠀴󠀹󠁽󠁛󠁤󠀳󠁝Drain, then rinse under the cold tap so they don’t stick.󠁻󠁤󠀳󠁽
  4. 󠁛󠀴󠁤󠁝󠁛󠀴󠁨󠁝30 seconds󠁻󠀴󠁨󠁽 after the end󠁻󠀴󠁤󠁽󠁛󠁤󠀵󠁝Toss with the tarator and a quarter of the seasoning.󠁻󠁤󠀵󠁽
  5. 󠁛󠀴󠁤󠁝󠁛󠀴󠁪󠁝1 minute󠁻󠀴󠁪󠁽 after the end󠁻󠀴󠁤󠁽󠁛󠁤󠀷󠁝Top with tomato, turnip, parsley and sumac. Eat it warm or cold.󠁻󠁤󠀷󠁽

󠁛󠀳󠁴󠁝Why it works󠁻󠀳󠁴󠁽

󠁛󠁤󠀹󠁝Rinsed noodles stay separate and take the tahini like a dressing, so the bowl tastes like the falafel sandwich.󠁻󠁤󠀹󠁽
