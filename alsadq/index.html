Infrastructure for communities.

AlSadq is a Syrian contracting company. We rehabilitate the water networks, sewers, wells and pumping stations communities depend on, bring them onto solar power, and restore the schools, bakeries and public buildings they serve, for local and international organisations.

01The structure beneath

Most of what we build, no one will see again.

Infrastructure, from the Latin infra, is the structure below. A water main is laid under a street and the street is closed over it. A well casing goes down further than anyone will look. A sewer runs where nobody walks. What a community sees is the result: water at the tap, bread from the oven, a classroom open, a street lit after dark.

Because the work disappears, its record must not. Each project below is listed with the organisation that commissioned or funded it, the place and the year, whenever the record gives them.

02Six areas of work

One system, read from the roof down.

Each area of our work is one part of the same system. The sun drives the pump, the pump lifts water to the tank, the network carries it to the school and the bakery, and the sewer takes it away to be treated.
