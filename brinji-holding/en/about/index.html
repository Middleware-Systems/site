Our Story

A family business since 1998.

BRINJI Holding is a family-owned business group founded by the Brinjikji brothers, in business since 1998. Its companies make, distribute and market, with offices in Syria, Türkiye and the UAE.

The group's primary focus is the production, distribution and trade of fast-moving consumer goods. It is managing partner for Indomie in Syria, and it carries the brands of international and regional food and beverage companies, among them Mondelēz International, Savola, Arla, Indofood, Pınar, Heinz, Dole and Barilla.

Behind those names stand a strong distribution network, operational know-how, and a commitment to innovation and sustainability. With them the group keeps doing what it has consistently done: delivering high-quality products that meet consumers' changing needs, creating value for its stakeholders, and building partnerships that last.

How the group works

One value chain. A distinct role for each company.

BRINJI Holding is a diversified group built around specialised subsidiaries, each with a distinct role in one integrated value chain. Its manufacturing companies produce food and beverages. Its distribution companies manage nationwide market access in consumer goods and healthcare. Its marketing and commercial companies support brand development, trade execution and partner relations.

This integrated model gives the group full control of quality, execution and market performance, and it is what lets the group build long-term partnerships with global and regional brands.
