Top Brands was established in late 2025 to serve the high-volume, price-sensitive segments of the Syrian market. It distributes consumer goods in the price-performance tier: accessible, good value and consistently available.

A flexible commercial model

Top Brands complements the group's wider distribution structure with a flexible model: a rapid response to the market, competitive pricing and strong turnover across every channel.

Across pricing tiers

With Top Brands, BRINJI Holding operates across multiple pricing tiers and meets the full spectrum of consumer demand, backed by efficient logistics, an agile sales force and a growing nationwide footprint.
