Founded in 2015, B2B Türkiye is the group's operational hub for consumer goods across Türkiye and beyond, and a strategic trade bridge between Syria and global markets.

What it does

The company specialises in multi-brand portfolio management in the domestic market, import and export coordination, and targeted distribution, with a strong focus on Türkiye's ethnic retail market.

The brands it carries

It delivers high-demand products from trusted international and regional brands, including Indomie, Jack Wrestler, Bronto, Diox, Kharta, Malto, Wild Power and Al Dar.
