SAWAB Factory has stood in Adra Industrial City, Damascus, since 2004. Its 23,000 m² plant produces more than 3.5 million packs a day, with raw-material warehouses, finished-goods storage and its own quality-control department on site.

Quality comes first

Raw materials are selected against legal requirements, global standards and the factory's own benchmarks. Every material is audited and tested to international standards in the factory's laboratories, by qualified staff.

Checked at every phase

Organoleptic tests are carried out at every phase of production. Storage and shipment follow Good Storage and Transfer Conditions, to protect the product's integrity.

All the way to the shelf

Field inspectors audit products at the points of sale, so the standard set in the factory still holds when the product reaches the consumer.
