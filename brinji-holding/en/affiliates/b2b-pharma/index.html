B2B Pharma is a licensed healthcare distribution company, officially authorised by the Syrian Ministry of Health and the Syrian Pharmacists Syndicate. It serves pharmacies, hospitals and medical centres with dietary supplements, baby care products and infant milk formulas, with a focus on reliable access across all Syrian governorates.

One partner. The full journey to market.

B2B Pharma brings regulatory coordination, quality-led handling and commercial execution into one accountable route to the Syrian healthcare market: a credible local platform for international healthcare brands to enter, operate and grow with confidence.

The partnership platform

  1. Regulatory pathway: registration support, documentation and local coordination
  2. Controlled operations: storage, temperature monitoring and inventory discipline
  3. Quality assurance: SOP-led processes, traceability and recall readiness
  4. Nationwide access: reliable delivery to pharmacies, hospitals and medical centres
  5. Commercial growth: launch execution, market feedback and transparent reporting
