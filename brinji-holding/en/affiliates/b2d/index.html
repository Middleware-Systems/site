Brinji to Drinks is the group's beverage manufacturer, established as part of its 2026 strategic expansion plan in Syria. From its plant in the Aleppo Industrial Area it produces juices, carbonated drinks, energy drinks and other beverages.

Three lines, three formats

A KHS canning line, a Tetra Pak carton line and a SASIB glass-bottling line let one plant make a wide range of beverage formats while holding one standard of quality, efficiency and reliability.

Our own brands

Bronto, Tropica and O Plus are BRINJI Holding's own brands. Each is positioned for a different consumer through its formats and price points.

Under licence

B2D has a brand licensing agreement with SGÜ, the owner of the energy drinks Jack Wrestler and Wild Power, to produce them in Syria. It is a growing platform for contract manufacturing, with plans to open its lines to more regional and international partners.
