B2B Syria

A distribution company at the centre of the group's work in Syria.

Role
Distribution
Sector

Country

Send an enquiryVisit b2b-fmcg.com

B2B Syria plays a central role in the group's operations in the Syrian market. It represents a portfolio of global and regional brands, among them Oreo, Cadbury, Milka, Lurpak, Puck, Kraft, Heinz, Dole, Barilla, Jack Wrestler, Afia and Clipper.

A full range in a single delivery

B2B keeps widening the range of products it can bring to a shop in one delivery. For its partners that means convenience and a seamless supply chain.

The network behind it

The group's direct distribution network covers Syria from 12 branches: 36,122 active points of sale, warehouses totalling 93,900 m² and 291 vehicles.
